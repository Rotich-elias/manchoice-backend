# Privacy Policy for MAN'S CHOICE ENTERPRISE Mobile Application

**Last Updated:** November 16, 2025

## 1. Introduction

MAN'S CHOICE ENTERPRISE ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services for credit management and loan processing.

By using our application, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Identification Information
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- National ID number
- Physical address
- Business name (if applicable)

### 2.2 Financial Information
- Credit limit details
- Loan application data (principal amount, interest rates, duration)
- Payment history and transaction records
- Credit scoring information
- Bank/M-PESA payment details

### 2.3 Document Images and Photos
- Passport-sized photographs
- National ID card (front and back)
- Motorcycle registration documents
- Logbook photos
- Guarantor identification documents
- Next of kin identification documents

### 2.4 Vehicle Information
- Motorcycle number plate
- Chassis number
- Vehicle model and type
- Engine capacity (CC)
- Vehicle color

### 2.5 Third-Party Information
- Next of kin details (name, phone, relationship, email)
- Guarantor details (name, phone, relationship, email, vehicle information)
- Guarantor identification documents

### 2.6 Technical Information
- Device information
- IP address
- Usage statistics
- Authentication tokens

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

### 3.1 Loan Processing and Management
- Verify your identity and eligibility for loans
- Process loan applications and approvals
- Calculate credit limits and risk assessment
- Manage payment schedules and collections
- Track loan repayment progress

### 2.2 Financial Services
- Process payments through M-PESA integration
- Generate payment receipts and statements
- Calculate interest and penalties
- Monitor account activity and creditworthiness

### 3.3 Customer Support and Communication
- Provide customer service and support
- Send important notifications about your account
- Communicate loan status updates
- Respond to inquiries and resolve issues

### 3.4 Security and Compliance
- Prevent fraud and unauthorized transactions
- Ensure compliance with financial regulations
- Maintain accurate records for auditing purposes
- Protect against security threats

### 3.5 Service Improvement
- Analyze usage patterns to improve our services
- Develop new features and functionality
- Enhance user experience and application performance

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information based on the following legal grounds:

- **Contractual Necessity:** Processing is necessary for the performance of our loan agreement with you
- **Legal Obligation:** Compliance with financial regulations and anti-money laundering laws
- **Legitimate Interests:** Fraud prevention, network security, and business operations
- **Consent:** For specific purposes where we obtain your explicit consent

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 Third-Party Service Providers
We may share your information with:
- **M-PESA/Safaricom:** For payment processing and transaction verification
- **Cloud storage providers:** For secure document storage
- **Technical support providers:** For application maintenance

### 5.2 Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information when required by:
- Court orders or legal processes
- Government authorities and regulatory bodies
- Law enforcement agencies for investigation purposes

### 5.3 Business Transfers
In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity.

### 5.4 With Your Consent
We may share your information with third parties when you have given us explicit consent to do so.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

### 6.1 Storage Location
Your data is stored on secure servers located in Kenya. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information.

### 6.2 Security Measures
- Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
- Secure authentication and access controls
- Regular security assessments and updates
- Employee training on data protection
- Physical security measures for server facilities

### 6.3 Data Retention
We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:
- Fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy
- Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
- Resolve disputes and enforce agreements

Typically, we retain:
- Loan application data: 7 years after loan completion
- Payment records: 7 years from transaction date
- Customer profiles: While account remains active plus 2 years
- Identification documents: 2 years after account closure

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

### 7.1 Access and Correction
- Right to access the personal information we hold about you
- Right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information

### 7.2 Data Portability
- Right to receive your personal information in a structured, commonly used format

### 7.3 Withdrawal of Consent
- Right to withdraw consent for processing, where applicable
- Note: Withdrawal may affect our ability to provide certain services

### 7.4 Deletion Rights
- Right to request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal requirements

### 7.5 Objection to Processing
- Right to object to processing of your personal information in certain circumstances

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided in Section 10.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Your personal information is processed and stored primarily within Kenya. If we transfer your data outside Kenya, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the application
- Sending notifications through the application
- Updating the "Last Updated" date

Your continued use of the application after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
