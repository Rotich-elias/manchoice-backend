Account Deletion Policy for MAN'S CHOICE ENTERPRISE Mobile Application

Last Updated: November 18, 2025

Introduction

MAN'S CHOICE ENTERPRISE ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have control over your personal data. This Account Deletion Policy explains how you can request the deletion of your account and associated data from our mobile application and related services.

How to Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your MAN'S CHOICE ENTERPRISE account and associated data, you can submit a deletion request by:

  • Email: Send your request to admin@manschoice.co.ke with the subject "Account Deletion Request"
  • In-App Support: Use the support feature within the mobile application
  • Written Request: Send a written request to our physical address: 7-30129 Chepkorio

Required Information for Deletion Request

To process your account deletion request, we require the following information to verify your identity:

  • Full name as registered in our system
  • Phone number associated with your account
  • National ID number used during registration
  • Email address linked to your account
  • Reason for account deletion (optional)

Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon successful verification and approval of your deletion request, we will permanently delete the following personal data:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone, address)
  • User account credentials and profile information
  • Uploaded document images (passport photos, ID cards, etc.)
  • Vehicle information and registration details
  • Next of kin and guarantor contact information
  • Application usage data and preferences

Data That May Be Retained

Due to legal and regulatory requirements in Kenya, we are required to retain certain financial and transaction data for specified periods:

  • Financial transaction records: Retained for 7 years as required by financial regulations
  • Loan application and approval data: Retained for 7 years for compliance and audit purposes
  • Payment history and receipts: Retained for 7 years for tax and regulatory compliance
  • Fraud prevention data: Retained as necessary for security and legal protection

Processing Timeline

We will process your account deletion request within 30 days of receiving all required verification information. You will receive confirmation via email once the deletion process is complete.

Important Considerations

  • Account deletion is irreversible and cannot be undone
  • You will lose access to all loan history and transaction records
  • Any pending loan applications will be cancelled
  • Active loans must be fully repaid before account deletion can be processed
  • You may need to re-register if you wish to use our services in the future
