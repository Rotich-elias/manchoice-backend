Release Notes

We are always working to make borrowing, repaying and managing your account simpler. Here is a short history of recent changes.

November 19, 2025

Meet the new website pages New

  • Added an About Us page with our story, mission and contact details.
  • Added a Products page covering loans, motorcycle parts and digital services.
  • One-tap app download links are now available on every page.
November 18, 2025

Account deletion, explained Privacy

  • Published a dedicated Account Deletion Policy with clear steps to submit a request.
  • Documented what data is deleted, what must be retained by law, and the 30-day processing timeline.
November 16, 2025

Our Privacy Policy is live Privacy

  • Read the full Privacy Policy for details on the information we collect and how we use it.
  • Added plain-language sections on your rights, data security and how to file a complaint.
November 6, 2025

Better account support for customers

  • Administrators can help customers reset a forgotten PIN.
  • Improved customer profile screens and onboarding flows.
November 5, 2025

Admin loan creation and polish

  • Staff can create loans on behalf of customers from the admin panel.
  • General interface refinements and a fix for an overnight scheduling error.
October 31, 2025

Late penalties and faster responses

  • Late payment penalties are now calculated automatically on overdue loans.
  • Added caching so common requests return faster.
